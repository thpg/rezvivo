Natural Earth - public domain geographic data
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

The offline globe uses Natural Earth I (3.2.0), shaded relief, water and
drainages, and the Natural Earth vector dataset (5.1.2): countries, populated
places, physical regions, marine regions, lakes and rivers. Natural Earth
raster and vector data are in the public domain.

Runtime resources: data/map-overview/world.rzmap and labels.json.
Exact source URLs and payload checksums: data/map-overview/manifest.json.
Rebuild tool: tools/build-map-overview.py. The installer ships the baked
resources, not the large source raster or GeoJSON files.
